# Root of All Dishes playtest, 8 Oct 2026

How it was tested: a fresh game played headlessly in Chromium with a scripted player that followed the goals, built what
Plan production asked for, then bought the French pack and found the tarte flambée. Two long runs went to about day 60 of
game time: one where the player only sold through orders, one with "Sell the surplus automatically" ticked everywhere.
Screens were checked at 1440, 1280 and 1024 px wide. No console errors in any run, and every item, dish and building has
its SVG icon.

## What works well

- The goal chain is smooth: the first 14 goals (water to salt) finished in about 9 minutes of game time with no dead ends,
  and each unlock arrives right when it is needed.
- Plan production, Flows and the "where the missing input comes from" hint carry the teaching well.
- The tarte flambée moment lands: big, warm, and it pays off the Shop's "one flatbread here is rather special" teaser.

## Bugs fixed in this pass

1. **Top bar cut off on smaller screens.** Between about 760 and 1225 px wide, Codex, Orders, Goals, Shop and the menu were
   pushed off the right edge ([screenshot](topbar-cut-off-1024px.png)). Below 1250 px the logo and the season/weather words
   now hide (icons and tooltips stay), and below 1000 px the bar wraps onto two rows.
2. **Wells with auto-sell pumped water forever.** The market doesn't buy water, so a Well with auto-sell ignored its stock
   target and piled up 34,674 water. It now stops at its target.
3. **The tarte flambée celebration always unpaused the game** (to normal speed) when closed. It now restores whatever
   speed you had.
4. **Market rows jumped under the cursor.** The Sell list re-sorted by stock value every second, so the 1 / 10 / All buttons
   could move between rows and sell the wrong thing. Rows now keep their place while the Market is open.

Downloads (foodsim.html and foodsim.zip) are rebuilt.

## Status: implemented (8 Oct, same day)

All ideas below are in the game except two: buildings still mostly unlock with the first pack you buy (that only happens
when a pack is bought before the Butcher & cheesemaker goal), and the top bar still shows icons only below 1500 px (keyboard
shortcuts were added instead, see the help screen). Balance changes: Field 50 coins + 0.3/min upkeep, Tropical Greenhouse
700 + 1/min, Quarry 350 + 0.5/min, packs +40% of base price per pack owned, hint cost scales with the dish's chain size.

## Improvement ideas, most valuable first

### 1. Show production rates and bottlenecks (mechanics + teaching)
The single most confusing moment: the Creamery's butter step sat on "Waiting for Cream" even though a cream step was
running ([screenshot](butter-waiting-for-cream.png)). One cream step makes 6 cream a minute; butter wants 12. The hint says
"Cream is made at Creamery", which you already have. Nothing in the game shows rates.
- Inspector: "makes 6/min, uses 30 milk/min".
- Missing-input hint: "Your 1 Creamery makes 6 cream/min, this step needs 12. Build another (💰550)".
- Warehouse: a made/used per minute column with red for items running down.
This is also the core supply-chain lesson: throughput, not just the recipe tree.

### 2. Auto-sell breaks stock targets for everything downstream
With auto-sell ticked a building never stops, so a Kitchen happily turns every butter into toast while other buildings and
the Test Kitchen starve. In the auto-sell run, butter was never in stock for 30 game days, which blocked discovering any
dish that uses it.
- Split the control into "keep N in stock" and "sell above N" so a building can sell surplus but still pause.
- Add a simple priority (high / normal / low) per building, or an input reserve ("leave 10 butter for others").

### 3. Discovery gets stuck in the mid-game
After the Village kitchen unlock there are 27 dishes in the Cookbook, but the Test Kitchen only lists what is in stock
right now, so you can't even try a combination until the whole chain is built and stocked.
- Show unlocked-but-empty ingredients greyed out in the Test Kitchen. Trying them could still discover the dish ("you'll
  need Butter to cook it") without cooking it.
- A "Try in Test Kitchen" button on Cookbook cards that pre-fills the revealed ingredients.
- Hint costs (30, 60, 90 coins) become trivial later; scale them with the dish's chain size.

### 4. Money has no floor
Upkeep keeps charging into the negative forever: the order-only run reached −3,258 with every building still running and
no consequence beyond a repeated toast. Options: a bank loan with interest, buildings with a cycle cost pausing below zero,
or a clear "in debt" state that blocks building and buying.

### 5. Economy balance
- Fields cost 40, have no upkeep and earn about 11 coins/min, the best return in the game. The Tropical Greenhouse (900 +
  3/min upkeep) nets about 4.6/min, and the Quarry about 1.2/min on a 500 cost, so they never pay back.
- Packs cost 2,500 to 6,000 but an auto-sell farm of 19 buildings reached 49,000 by day 62, so after the first one or two
  packs money stops mattering. Escalating pack prices (each one dearer than the last) would keep the Shop a real choice.

### 6. Orders could drive discovery
Orders only ask for things you already make, mostly for 30 to 100 coins. Occasional special requests for an undiscovered
dish ("The Grand Hotel wants Croque Monsieur") or bulk contracts ("School canteen: 20 loaves") would give the mid-game
direction and teach scale.

### 7. Smaller UX polish
- Below 1500 px the 9 top-bar buttons are icon-only. Labels on the three most used (Test Kitchen, Cookbook, Shop) or
  keyboard shortcuts in the tooltips would help newcomers.
- Fields show nothing until the crop is 45% grown, so a fresh wheat field looks the same as an idle one. A small crop badge
  like other buildings have would fix it.
- Toasts cover the bottom of the inspector's recipe list, and big unlocks fire a stack of "New ingredient made" toasts.
  Group them into one.
- Space toggles pause and normal speed, so it drops you out of fast mode.
- The first cuisine pack unlocks almost every building at once (French adds 7); after that every pack shows "1 new building:
  Pasta workshop". Spreading buildings across goals and packs would keep each purchase feeling new.
- The "Butcher & cheesemaker" stage advertises cheese, but Cheddar already unlocks with the Village kitchen, so the "Make
  a cheese" goal can be done early. Either move Cheddar or adjust the stage description.

### 8. Teaching extras
- An end-of-day summary: "Your farm turned 40 wheat into 30 loaves; the farmer's share was 15%".
- A winter warning a day ahead: "Hay in stock lasts your cows about 4 minutes of winter".
