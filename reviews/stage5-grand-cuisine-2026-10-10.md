## Notes

- **Haute Cuisine is pricey but not long.** Its chains are about average length (17 items). If it should also teach long chains, the dishes could gain refined ingredients such as a demi-glace or a beurre blanc base. That would be a data change for later.
- **Pâtisserie is the strongest fit.** Chocolate, laminated butter doughs and sugar work give it the longest chains of all three.
- **Grand Banquets spans the world**, so the final stage isn't only European: Peking Duck, sushi and kaiseki dishes, biryani, Persian rice, mole, feijoada and doro wat.
- **Effect on stages 1 to 4.** 46 of these 70 dishes were in a cuisine of the earlier 3-stage draft. French loses 12, Japanese 7, and Italian and Chinese & Korean 5 each. The other 24 come from the free pool. Stages 1 to 4 will need rebalancing once this is settled.
