# Five stages, 15 cuisines (draft, 2026-10-10)

On paper only, no code changes yet. All 450 existing dishes are placed and none is left over. Each stage has 3 cuisines; the player picks the order inside a stage and moves on once all 3 are done. Stage E, Grand Cuisine, is the fixed 70 from [stage5-grand-cuisine-2026-10-10.md](stage5-grand-cuisine-2026-10-10.md).

## Notes

- **The Americas is the biggest cuisine at 84 dishes**, almost twice East Asian (44). That fits "can differ in total count", but if it feels too long it splits cleanly into North America (53) and Latin America & Caribbean (31). That would make 4 cuisines in Stage D, or one could move to Stage C.
- **Around the World** collects Eastern Europe and the Caucasus, Africa, and Australia and New Zealand. None of these has enough dishes for its own cuisine.
- **Swiss Alpine is its own early cuisine.** Its chains are short (cheese, potatoes, barley) and it gives a home-grown start.
- **Breakfast dishes are spread out.** Waffles and Crêpes go to French & Belgian; Pancakes, French Toast, Avocado Toast and Smoothie go to The Americas, so Farmhouse Kitchen stays small.
- **Drinks are spread by origin**: tea and elderflower to British, espresso and cappuccino to Italian, sangria and mint tea to Mediterranean, mulled wine to German, green tea and matcha to East Asian, chai and lassi to South Asian, coffee drinks, hot chocolate and cocktails to The Americas.
